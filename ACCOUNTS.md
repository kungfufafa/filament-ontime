## 🛡️ Pembagian Role & Hak Akses

| Role | Akses & Wewenang | Pengajuan & Approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Superadmin** | Akses Penuh Admin Panel Filament (Policy, Company, Users, Approval Flow) | Approval Akhir (Stage 3) |
| **BOD** | Board of Directors / Akses Manajemen Eksekutif | Approval Stage 2 |
| **Approver** | Manager / Coordinator Divisi | Approval Stage 1 (Verifikasi Divisi) |
| **Employee** | Panel Absensi Karyawan (Check-In/Check-Out, Cuti, Lembur, Koreksi) | Melakukan Pengajuan |
